Freezer Inventory
Track frozen meals, leftovers, and bulk items. See what's expiring, categorise by type, and never wonder "what's actually in the freezer?" again. Lives right in your Supplies tab alongside Shopping and Pantry.
New
⚡
Instant Sync
Changes now appear instantly across all devices. No more pull-to-refresh. Mark a chore done on your phone and the kiosk updates in real time.
Improved
✍️
Edit Recipes
Full recipe editing — update ingredients, steps, servings, and more. Ingredient quantities now auto-parse from freetext too.
New
👪
Multi-Kid Routines & Allowances
Assign routines and record allowance transactions for multiple kids at once. No more creating the same morning checklist one kid at a time — select everyone who needs it and save once.
New
🛒
Smart Shopping List
Duplicate detection merges quantities automatically. Add "milk" when it's already there and it just bumps the count.
New

Quick Switcher
Tap the household badge to switch between homes instantly. Perfect for separated families or sharehouse managers.
New
🔒
Easier Login
No more PINs. Members request access, managers approve via email. Device loads automatically.

Feedback You Can Track
Filter to see your own submissions, get email notifications when your feedback is acknowledged or resolved, and reply directly to feedback threads.
