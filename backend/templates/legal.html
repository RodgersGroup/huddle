Getting Started with Huddle

Huddle is your household management app — chores, meals, calendar, bills, shopping, and more, all in one place. Here's how to get going:

1Create your account
Enter your email at the login screen. We'll send you a magic link — no password needed! Tap the link in your email to log in securely.
2Set up your household
Choose a name for your household, add your display name, and pick a colour. Then add your household members — they'll each get their own profile.
3Choose your modules
Toggle on the features you need: chores, meal planning, calendar, bills, shopping, pantry, polls, and more. You can always change these later in settings.
4Invite your household
Share your invite code or link with your household members. They'll be able to join your household and start collaborating straight away.
5Install the app
Huddle works as a progressive web app (PWA). On your phone, tap "Add to Home Screen" in your browser menu to install it like a native app. You'll get push notifications and offline access.

Optional: Kiosk Display

Got a Raspberry Pi or spare screen? Set up a kiosk display that shows an always-on household dashboard. Pair it using a QR code from the settings menu — no login needed on the kiosk itself.

Key Features

  • Chores — Rotating schedules, streaks, load balancing, and completion tracking
  • Meals — Weekly meal planning with variant support for split dinners
  • Calendar — Shared family calendar with recurring events
  • Bills — Track bills with due dates and recurring auto-generation
  • Shopping — Collaborative shopping list with purchase tracking
  • Pantry — Inventory tracking with low-stock alerts
  • Recipes — Save, import from URL, and add ingredients to shopping
  • Polls — Quick household votes for group decisions
  • Tasks — Ad-hoc personal or request tasks

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in?
We use magic links — enter your email and we'll send you a login link. No passwords to remember! The link expires after 10 minutes for security.
Can I belong to multiple households?
Yes! You can create or join multiple households. Use the household switcher in settings to swap between them.
What are the different roles?
Managers have full access including settings, member management, and admin features. Members can use all modules and complete tasks. Dependants have restricted access suited for younger household members.
How do chore rotations work?
When you assign multiple people to a chore, it automatically rotates to the next person after each completion. The system also load-balances to avoid giving one person too many chores in a day.
Can I get push notifications?
Yes! After installing the app, you'll be prompted to enable notifications. You'll get reminders for overdue chores, meal plans, upcoming events, and more. Notification times are configurable in settings.
Is my data backed up?
Yes, the database is automatically backed up every 6 hours with 30-day retention. Backups are compressed and stored securely on the server.
How do I import recipes?
In the Recipes module, tap "Import from URL" and paste any recipe URL. Huddle extracts the recipe data automatically from sites that use structured data (most major recipe sites). You can then add ingredients directly to your shopping list.
What is the kiosk display?
The kiosk is an always-on display (typically a Raspberry Pi with a screen) that shows your household dashboard — today's chores, upcoming events, meal plan, and weather. It updates in real-time and is great for a kitchen or hallway display.
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
Use the Feedback module in the app! You can submit bug reports, feature requests, general feedback, or praise. Your submissions are reviewed by the development team.
Can I delete my account?
Please contact us at keiran@rodgersgroup.au to request account deletion. We'll remove your user data, household memberships, and any associated personal information.

Privacy Policy

Effective: February 2025

What We Collect

Huddle collects the minimum data necessary to provide the service:

  • Email address — Used for authentication (magic link login) and account identification
  • Display name — Chosen by you, shown to other household members
  • Profile photo — Optional, uploaded by you
  • Household data — Chores, meals, events, bills, shopping lists, and other module data you create
  • Device information — User agent string, stored with feedback submissions for debugging
  • Location — Optional, used for weather forecasts and fuel prices if you choose to share it
  • Push subscription — Browser push notification endpoint, if you enable notifications

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the Huddle household management service
  • To authenticate your identity via magic link emails
  • To send push notifications you've opted into (chore reminders, event alerts)
  • To display weather and fuel prices based on your chosen location
  • To improve the service based on feedback and usage patterns

Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored in an SQLite database on a server located in Australia
  • External access is secured via HTTPS through Cloudflare Tunnel
  • Session cookies are signed and HTTP-only
  • The database is backed up every 6 hours with 30-day retention
  • Profile images are stored locally on the server, not on third-party services

Third-Party Services

  • Resend — Email delivery for magic links and notifications
  • Cloudflare — Tunnel and DNS for secure external access
  • Open-Meteo — Weather data (no personal data sent, only coordinates)
  • NSW OneGov — Fuel prices (no personal data sent, only coordinates)
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — Reverse geocoding during onboarding

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your household data is only visible to members of your household.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting keiran@rodgersgroup.au.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact: keiran@rodgersgroup.au
