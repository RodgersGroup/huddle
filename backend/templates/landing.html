Everything your household needs

Huddle replaces sticky notes, group chats, shared spreadsheets, and good intentions. Pick the modules that suit your household.

Built for Every Household

Not just for nuclear families

Huddle works for any group of people sharing a living space. Choose a preset during setup and Huddle enables the right features for your situation.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Families
Two parents, kids, the whole package. Chore rosters with fair rotation, morning and bedtime routines, rewards and allowances to teach responsibility, and a shared calendar everyone can see.
🏠
Split Families
Kids across two homes. Each household runs its own Huddle with its own routines, chores, and meal plans. Kids can be members of both, keeping consistency between houses.
🛋️
Sharehouses
Fair chore rotation so nobody gets stuck with the bins every week. Split bills, shared shopping lists, and polls for group decisions. No more passive-aggressive group chat messages.
💑
Couples
Meal planning, shared calendars, shopping lists, and a simple way to split household tasks without the mental load falling on one person.
👵
Multi-Generational
Grandparents, parents, and kids under one roof. Everyone sees what needs doing today. The kiosk display in the kitchen keeps everyone in the loop, no phone required.
👤
Solo & Couples
Living alone or as a couple? Huddle works as a personal household manager too. Stay on top of bills, meals, and chores — and add people later when your situation changes.

During setup, you choose a preset — Family, Sharehouse, Couple, or Just the Basics — and Huddle enables the right modules. You can always change what's turned on later.

Privacy & Security

Your data stays in Australia

Huddle is built and hosted entirely in Australia. Your household data is not a product — it's private, and it stays that way.

🇦🇺

Australian infrastructure

Your data is stored on Australian-owned servers, not on AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure. It never leaves the country.

🚫

No ads, ever

We never sell, share, or mine your data for advertising. No trackers, no analytics packages selling your behaviour to third parties.

🔐

Passwordless login

No passwords to leak or forget. Huddle uses magic link email authentication — a unique, time-limited link sent to your inbox.

💾

Backed up every 6 hours

Your data is backed up automatically throughout the day. In the unlikely event of a problem, we can restore quickly.

🏠

Household isolation

Every API call, every WebSocket message, every database query is scoped to your household. Other households cannot see your data.

📜

Transparent policies

Our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are written in plain English, not legalese. Read them in 5 minutes.

How It Works

Up and running in 5 minutes

No downloads, no app store, no complicated setup. Just open the link and go.

1
Open the link
Visit huddle.rodgersgroup.au in your phone's browser. Enter your email to receive a login link.
2
Create your household
Name your household, add your people, and choose a preset. Huddle enables the right features for you.
3
Install the app
Tap "Add to Home Screen" and Huddle appears on your phone like any other app. Allow notifications to get branded reminders for chores, meals, events, and more.
4
Invite your household
Share the household code with your members. They enter the code, pick their name, and you approve them from your email. No PINs or passwords needed.
Common Questions

Quick answers

Is Huddle really free?

Yes. During the beta, every feature is unlocked at no cost. When paid plans launch later, you'll get at least 30 days notice and a loyalty discount. A free tier with core modules will always exist for every household type.

Do I need to download it from the App Store?

No. Huddle is a web app that installs directly from your browser. Visit the link, tap "Add to Home Screen", and it works like any other app. No App Store or Google Play required.

How many people can be in a household?

It depends on your plan — from 1 (Solo & Couples free) to 8+ (Home+ or Share+). During the beta, there's no limit. Each person gets their own profile, colour, and role. See Pricing for details.

Can kids use it without an email address?

Yes. Only the person setting up the household needs an email. Children and dependents can join by entering the household code on their device — a manager just approves them via email. No passwords or PINs required.

How do other members log in?

Members tap "Request access", enter your household code, pick their name (or type a new one), and you get an email to approve. Their device loads automatically once approved. Managers log in via magic link email.

What happens to my data?

Your data is stored on Australian servers, backed up every 6 hours, and never sold or shared. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details — it's written in plain English.
