Beta Tester Agreement

Effective 13 February 2026 · Version 1.0

Welcome to the Huddle Beta

Thank you for helping us test Huddle. By signing up during the beta period, you are joining as an early tester. This agreement explains what you can expect from us and what we ask of you.

The short version: You get full access to every Huddle feature at no cost. All we ask is that you tell us what works, what doesn't, and what you would like to see.

What You Get

Full Access

Every module and feature is unlocked during the beta. There are no paywalls, no feature gates, and no usage limits. You have the same access as someone on the highest paid plan.

No Cost

The beta is completely free. You will never be charged without your explicit consent. When paid plans are introduced after the beta, you will receive advance notice and a loyalty discount.

Early Influence

Your feedback directly shapes the product. Features that beta testers ask for are prioritised. You can vote on other testers' suggestions to help us decide what to build next.

Beta Tester Loyalty Discount

When Huddle moves to commercial pricing, beta testers will receive a discount on paid plans as a thank you. Details will be announced before any charges take effect.

What We Ask of You

  1. Provide feedback. Use the in-app Feedback module to report bugs, suggest features, share what you like, and tell us what frustrates you. This is the most valuable thing you can do.
  2. Be honest. We would rather hear that something is broken or confusing than hear nothing at all. Constructive criticism helps us build a better product.
  3. Be patient. Things may break. Features may change. We are actively developing and improving the app. We will fix issues as quickly as we can.
  4. Spread the word. If you know someone who would benefit from Huddle, let them know. More testers means more feedback, which means a better product for everyone.

What to Expect

During the beta

Huddle is under active development. You may encounter bugs, incomplete features, or occasional downtime. We take reliability seriously and aim for minimal disruption, but we cannot guarantee perfect uptime during this phase.

We may make changes to features, the interface, or how things work based on feedback and testing. Where possible, we will let you know about significant changes in advance.

Your data

Your household data is real and persistent. We back it up every six hours and treat it with the same care as production data. In the unlikely event that we need to reset or migrate data, we will give you advance notice and help you through the process.

Transition to commercial

When the beta ends, we will:

  1. Give you at least 30 days notice before any paid plans take effect.
  2. Provide a loyalty discount for beta testers on all paid plans.
  3. Ensure a free plan remains available for every household type with core modules, so nobody loses access to the basics.
  4. Never delete your data or lock you out because the beta ended.

Privacy and Data

Your data is stored on Australian-owned infrastructure, not on third-party cloud servers. We never sell, share, or use your data for advertising. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Your feedback submissions may be reviewed by the development team. We will never share your personal information or household data publicly.

Other Terms

This agreement is supplementary to the Huddle Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which also apply during the beta period.
