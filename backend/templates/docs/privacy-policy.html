Privacy Policy

Effective 13 February 2026 · Version 1.0

Overview

Huddle ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Rodgers Group. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, how we store it, and what rights you have.

What We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the Huddle service.

InformationPurpose
Email addressAccount creation and passwordless login (magic link authentication). Only required for the person creating or joining a household. Dependents such as children do not need an email address.
Display nameIdentifying household members within the app.
Household dataChores, calendar events, meals, recipes, shopping lists, bills, tasks, routines, and other content you create within the app. This data is created by you and is necessary for the service to function.
Push notification tokensDelivering reminders and notifications to your device. You can disable notifications at any time.
Feedback submissionsBug reports, feature requests, and suggestions you submit through the in-app Feedback module.
Device informationBrowser type and basic device information (user agent string) for troubleshooting and compatibility purposes.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, browsing history, or any data from other apps on your device.

How We Store Your Data

Your household data is stored on Australian-owned server infrastructure operated by Rodgers Group. It is not stored on third-party cloud platforms such as Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, or Microsoft Azure.

When you access Huddle remotely (outside your home network), your connection is secured using Cloudflare Tunnel, which provides end-to-end encryption. Cloudflare acts as a secure relay and does not store your household data.

The database is backed up automatically every six hours. Backups are stored on the same infrastructure and are retained for 30 days.

How We Use Your Data

We use your personal information solely to provide and improve the Huddle service. Specifically:

UseDetails
Providing the serviceDisplaying your household data, sending notifications, and enabling collaboration between household members.
AuthenticationSending magic link emails to verify your identity when you log in.
Improving HuddleReviewing feedback submissions and aggregated, non-identifying usage patterns to improve features and fix bugs.
SupportResponding to support requests you send to us.

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. We do not display advertisements in Huddle. We do not use your data to build profiles for targeted advertising.

Children's Privacy

Huddle can be used by households that include children. Dependents (including children under 18) can be added to a household by a manager (parent or guardian) without providing an email address. Children's data within the app (chore completions, routine progress, allowance balances) is managed by the household's managers and is subject to the same protections as all other household data.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental involvement.

Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

RightHow to exercise it
Access your dataYou can view all your household data within the app at any time.
Correct your dataYou can edit your profile, household information, and all content within the app.
Delete your dataYou can delete your account and all associated data by contacting us at huddle@rodgersgroup.au. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
ComplainIf you believe we have breached the APPs, you can contact us or lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days (to allow for backup rotation).

Feedback submissions may be retained in anonymised form after account deletion to help us improve the product.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, modification, and disclosure. These measures include encrypted connections (TLS/SSL), session-based authentication with signed tokens, rate limiting on login endpoints, and regular automated backups.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected users and the OAIC in accordance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by email before the changes take effect. The "Effective" date at the top of this document indicates when it was last updated.
