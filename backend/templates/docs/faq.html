Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Huddle.

Getting Started
What is Huddle?

Huddle is a household management app. It puts chores, routines, meals, recipes, calendars, shopping lists, bills, and more into one app that everyone in your household can use from their phone.

It replaces the whiteboard on the fridge, the shared spreadsheet nobody updates, and the group chat arguments about whose turn it is.

Is Huddle free?

Right now, yes. Huddle is in free open beta. Every feature and module is unlocked at no cost. All we ask is that you provide feedback through the in-app Feedback module so we can improve the product.

When paid plans launch after the beta, pricing depends on your household type. Solo & Couples plans start at $2.99/mo, Household plans from $7.99/mo, and Sharehouse plans from $4.99/mo. A free tier with core modules will always exist for every household type. Beta testers will receive advance notice and a loyalty discount.

How do I install it?

Visit huddle.rodgersgroup.au in your phone's browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android). Log in with your email, and your phone will prompt you to "Add to Home Screen." Tap that and the Huddle icon will appear on your phone like any other app.

There is nothing to download from the App Store or Google Play right now. Native app store versions are planned for after the beta.

Does everyone in my household need an email address?

No. Only the person creating the household needs an email address. Other members who want to log in from their own phone will need their own email. But dependents (such as children) can be added to the household by a manager without needing an email address at all.

What phones does it work on?

Huddle works on any phone with a modern web browser. This includes iPhones (Safari), Android phones (Chrome), and tablets. It also works in a desktop browser if you prefer.

How do I invite people to my household?

Go to Settings, then Household, then "Invite Members." You will get an invite code that you can share with your household members by text or in person. They visit huddle.rodgersgroup.au, enter the code, and set up their profile.

Features
How does the chore rotation work?

When you create a chore, you assign it to one or more people and set a schedule (daily, specific days, or every X days). Huddle automatically rotates whose turn it is. If someone completes a chore, the next person in the rotation is assigned next time.

Huddle also tracks streaks (consecutive completions) and load-balances across the household so nobody gets overwhelmed.

What are routines?

Routines are daily checklists, typically for children. You can set up a morning routine (brush teeth, get dressed, pack bag) and a bedtime routine (homework, shower, set out tomorrow's clothes). The checklist resets each day. Kids can check items off from the app, and parents can see progress.

Can I import recipes from websites?

Yes. Tap the Recipes tile, then "Import from URL." Paste the link from any recipe website and Huddle will pull in the recipe name, ingredients, and instructions. You can then link recipes to your meal plan or send ingredients straight to the shopping list.

Does the calendar sync with Google Calendar or Outlook?

No, and this is intentional. Huddle's calendar is dedicated to your household. It shows the events that matter to everyone at home: bin night, school pickup, family dinner, vet appointments. It is not cluttered with everyone's individual work meetings and reminders.

If you want to see a Huddle event in your personal calendar, you can manually add it there. But Huddle's calendar is designed to be a clean, focused view of your household's life.

Can different people have different meals on the same night?

Yes. The meal planner supports split meals. If one person is having something different (dietary requirements, fussy eaters, or just different preferences), you can set a variant for that person or group.

How do I choose which features to turn on?

During setup, Huddle asks what type of household you are (Family, Sharehouse, Couple, or Minimal) and enables the right modules. After that, you can toggle any module on or off from Settings. Disabling a module hides its tile from the home screen. Your data is kept even if you turn a module off.

Privacy and Data
Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored on Australian-owned server infrastructure operated by Rodgers Group. It is not stored on third-party cloud platforms like Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, or Microsoft Azure.

When you access Huddle from outside your home, your connection is encrypted using Cloudflare Tunnel.

Do you sell my data?

No. We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. We do not display ads in Huddle. We do not use your data to build advertising profiles. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Contact us at huddle@rodgersgroup.au and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data. Deletion is completed within 30 days to allow for backup rotation.

Is my data backed up?

Yes. The database is automatically backed up every six hours. Backups are retained for 30 days.

Beta Programme
What does "beta" mean?

It means Huddle is real and working, but still under active development. You may encounter the occasional bug or rough edge. We are actively improving the app based on feedback from testers like you.

Will I be charged when the beta ends?

No. You will never be charged without your explicit consent. When paid plans are introduced, we will give you at least 30 days notice and a loyalty discount. A free tier will continue to exist for every household type, so you will not lose access to the basics.

What household type should I pick?

Solo — if you live alone or with a partner (1–2 people). Covers all the essentials for managing your space.

Household — if you are a family, couple with kids, or multi-generational household. Unlocks family-focused modules like routines, rewards, and allowances.

Sharehouse — if you live with flatmates or uni housemates. Unlocks expense splitting, house rules, and tenancy management.

You can switch household type at any time from Settings. Your data is preserved when you switch.

Can I switch from Sharehouse to Household (or vice versa)?

Yes, at any time. Go to Settings and change your household type. Your data, members, and enabled modules are all preserved. The only thing that changes is which extra modules are available to you and which plan options you see.

How do I provide feedback?

Tap the Feedback tile on the home screen. You can submit bug reports, feature requests, general suggestions, or just tell us what you like. You can also vote on other people's feedback to help us prioritise. Alternatively, email us at huddle@rodgersgroup.au.

Will my data carry over after the beta?

Yes. Your household, chores, meals, recipes, and everything else will carry over when the beta transitions to commercial. We will not wipe or reset your data.

Kitchen Display (Optional)
Do I need the smart display?

No. The app works perfectly on its own from your phone. The smart display is an optional add-on that puts an always-on household dashboard in your kitchen or living area. It is useful but not required.

What is the smart display?

It is a small computer (Raspberry Pi 5) connected to a screen. It runs Huddle's kiosk mode automatically when powered on, showing today's chores, meals, calendar events, and weather. You pair it with your household by scanning a QR code from your phone — no login or keyboard needed on the display itself.

Can I use my own screen?

Yes. The Smart Display Kit ($369) includes everything except the screen. You supply your own monitor or TV with an HDMI input. We recommend a 24" IPS monitor with a pivot stand, such as the Dell P2425H (~$260) or the BenQ GW2490T (~$200), so you can use it in portrait or landscape. Any screen with HDMI will work.
