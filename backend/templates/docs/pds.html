What is Huddle?

Huddle is a household management app that keeps everyone in your home on the same page. Chore rosters, shared calendars, meal plans, recipes, shopping lists, routines, bills, and more, all in one app that every household member can access from their phone.

No more sticky notes on the fridge, group chat arguments about whose turn it is, or forgotten bills. Huddle replaces all of that with a single, simple app.

Huddle is currently available as an installable web app (PWA) that works on any phone with a browser. We are planning native iOS and Android apps for release after the beta period. An optional smart display add-on lets you mount a screen in your kitchen for an always-on household dashboard.

Designed and built in Australia, for Australian households.

Huddle is currently in free beta. All features are unlocked at no cost. All we ask is that you provide feedback through the in-app Feedback module so we can improve the system before commercial launch.

Who is Huddle for?

Huddle works for any group of people sharing a living space. Families keeping everyone organised, sharehouses fairly splitting chores and bills, couples coordinating daily life, and solo dwellers keeping on top of things. During setup, you choose your household type — Solo, Household, or Sharehouse — and Huddle enables the right features for your situation.

The App

Huddle is an app you install on your phone from the browser. It looks and feels like a native app, with its own icon on your home screen, push notifications, and offline support. It works on iPhone, Android, and any device with a modern browser.

The app features a tile-based home screen where each enabled module appears as a card with live status information. Tap a tile to open that module. A light and dark theme toggle lets you switch between modes. Everything updates in real time across all household members' phones via live sync.

After the beta period, Huddle will also be available as a native app on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Privacy and Data

Your household data belongs to you.

Your data is not stored on third-party cloud servers. Huddle runs on Australian-owned infrastructure. Your household information is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. Remote access from your phone is secured with an encrypted tunnel using the same technology banks and government agencies rely on.

We never sell, share, or use your household data for advertising. Huddle is compliant with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988.

You can export all your data or delete your account at any time. When you delete your data, it is permanently removed.
