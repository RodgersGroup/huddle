Getting Started

You'll be up and running in about 5 minutes.

Step 1 · 1 minute

Open Huddle

  1. On your phone, open your web browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android).
  2. Go to huddle.rodgersgroup.au
  3. You will see the Huddle login screen.
Step 2 · 1 minute

Log In

  1. Enter your email address and tap "Send Login Link".
  2. Check your email inbox. Open the message from Huddle and tap the login link.
  3. You are now logged in. No password needed.
Tip: Can't find the email? Check your spam or junk folder. The email comes from noreply@huddle.rodgersgroup.au. If it hasn't arrived after 2 minutes, try sending another one.
Step 3 · 3 minutes

Set Up Your Household

If you're the first person setting up, choose "Create a household". If someone else has already set up, skip to Step 4.

  1. Enter a name for your household (your address, family name, or anything you like).
  2. Enter your name and pick a colour that represents you.
  3. Add the other people in your household. Enter each person's name and choose their role: Manager, Member, or Dependent.
  4. Choose your household type: Solo & Couples (living alone or as a couple), Household (families and couples with kids), or Sharehouse (flatmates and uni students). This determines which extra modules are available and which plans you can choose. You can always change this later.
  5. Tap "Done". Your household is ready.
Tip: If you chose the Household type and added dependents, Huddle will automatically enable Routines, Rewards and Stars, and Allowances. These are designed to help kids and teenagers participate in running the household.
Step 4 · 1 minute

Join an Existing Household

If someone has already created a household and added your name, here's how to join:

  1. Go to huddle.rodgersgroup.au and enter the household code (the manager can find this in Settings > Household).
  2. Pick your name from the list of household members.
  3. A manager in the household will receive an email to approve your access. They just need to tap the link.
  4. Once approved, your page will automatically update and you're in. No need to refresh.
Tip: The approval email is sent instantly. Ask the manager to check their inbox (or spam folder) right away so you don't have to wait long.
Step 5 · 1 minute

Install the App

  1. Your phone should show a banner saying "Add to Home Screen". Tap it.
  2. If you don't see the banner: on iPhone, tap the Share button (square with arrow) then "Add to Home Screen". On Android, tap the three-dot menu then "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".
  3. The Huddle icon will appear on your phone's home screen. Tap it to open Huddle like any other app.
  4. When prompted, allow notifications so you receive chore reminders, meal alerts, and task requests.
Tip: Huddle works as an app on your phone but is installed from the browser, so there is nothing to download from the App Store or Google Play yet. You can switch between light and dark themes from the home screen.
Step 6 · 1 minute

Invite Your Household

  1. In Huddle, tap your avatar (top-right) to open Settings.
  2. Tap Household, then tap "Invite Members".
  3. Share the household code with your household members via text or in person.
  4. Each person visits huddle.rodgersgroup.au, enters the code, picks their name, and waits for a manager to approve.
  5. They can then install the app on their phone using the same method from Step 5.
🎉

You're All Set!

Huddle is running. Everyone can access it from their phone. Your home screen is organised into sections to help you find things quickly:

Daily Life
Chores, tasks, routines, and the family calendar.
Food & Kitchen
Meal planning, recipes, shopping lists, and pantry inventory.
Money
Bills, expenses, allowances, and rewards.
Family
Pets, homework assignments, polls, and the noticeboard.
Home
Tenancy, vehicles, house rules, feedback, and fuel prices.

Tap any tile to open it. Everything updates in real-time across all devices.

Optional

Add a Kitchen Display

If you have a Huddle Smart Display Kit, you can set up an always-on dashboard in your kitchen. This is entirely optional and the app works perfectly without it.

  1. Connect the HDMI cable from the Pi to your display and plug both into power.
  2. Wait about 60 seconds for the Huddle pairing screen to appear with a QR code.
  3. On your phone, scan the QR code (or go to the URL shown on screen).
  4. Enter your email address. Huddle will send you a login link.
  5. Once logged in, enter the 6-character code shown on the display and tap "Pair Display".
  6. The display will automatically connect to your household and show the dashboard.

Troubleshooting

Login email not arriving
Check your spam or junk folder. The email comes from noreply@huddle.rodgersgroup.au. If it still hasn't arrived after 2 minutes, try sending another one.
Access request not being approved
The manager needs to tap the approval link in their email. Ask them to check their inbox and spam folder. If the link has expired (24 hours), submit a new request from the login page.
App not installing on phone
Make sure you are visiting huddle.rodgersgroup.au in your phone's browser (not inside another app like Facebook or Instagram). On iPhone, use Safari. On Android, use Chrome.
Notifications not working
Open Huddle, go to Settings, and check that notifications are enabled. On iPhone, also check Settings > Notifications and make sure Huddle is allowed.
Display not pairing
Make sure the display and your phone are on the same network. The pairing code expires after 10 minutes. If it has expired, the display will show a new code automatically.
