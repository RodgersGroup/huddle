Everything you need to run your household

Over 20 modules that cover daily life. Toggle on what you need, turn off what you don't.

Chores

Rotating schedules, streaks, load balancing, and celebration animations. Fair for everyone.

Meals

Weekly meal planning with variant support. Different dinners for different people? No worries.

Calendar

Shared household calendar with recurring events, multi-day spans, and colour-coded participants.

Bills

Track bills with due dates, categories, and automatic recurrence. Never miss a payment.

Shopping

Collaborative shopping lists with categories, barcode scanning, and smart duplicate detection.

Recipes

Save recipes, import from any URL, scale servings, and add ingredients straight to shopping.

Self Care

Medication tracking, personal care reminders, and life admin with ADHD-friendly "last done" tracking.

House Rules

Shared household agreements everyone acknowledges. Set expectations for noise, guests, and common areas.

Vehicles

Rego and insurance reminders, service history, and maintenance scheduling. Never miss a renewal.

Plus routines, tasks, polls, pantry, freezer, noticeboard, assignments, allowances, rewards, pets, tenancy, expenses, feedback, and more.

Up and running in minutes

No app store download required. Huddle is a progressive web app that installs from your browser.

1

Create your household

Sign up with your email. No password needed, we use magic link authentication.

2

Add your members

Add everyone who lives with you. Assign roles: managers, members, or dependants.

3

Choose your modules

Toggle on what you need. Chores, meals, bills, shopping, calendar, and more.

4

Start collaborating

Share your invite link. Everyone can install the app and get push notifications.
